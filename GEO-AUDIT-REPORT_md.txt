## Executive Summary

**Overall GEO Score: 52/100 (Poor)**

LiLai Ireland is a well-crafted, genuinely expert site undermined by two structural problems for AI search. First — and most damaging — the site **blocks every major AI crawler** in robots.txt (ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent) via Cloudflare's "managed content" defaults, with a `Content-Signal: ai-train=no`. This means Claude, ChatGPT training, and Gemini grounding are told *not* to read or learn from the site — the exact opposite of GEO's goal. Second, as a ~6-month-old brand it has **almost no third-party footprint**: a search for the brand surfaces only competitors (ioutback, 葛瑞特, StudyDIY, ed:m), never LiLai itself.

The good news: the underlying content and expertise are strong. The founders are real, credentialed (Alex — UCD MSc Computer Science; Arsha — TCD MSc Digital Marketing + TESOL), the blog is deep, chaptered, and full of exactly the specific, quotable answers AI systems love. The site is server-rendered with a clean Rank Math schema baseline. Fix the crawler policy, complete the entity schema, and mark up the existing Q&A content, and this site can move from "Poor" to "Good" quickly — the raw material is already there.

## Critical Issues (Fix Immediately)

### 1. All major AI crawlers are blocked in robots.txt
The `/robots.txt` served via Cloudflare Managed Content explicitly disallows the crawlers that feed AI answers:

```
User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes,ai-train=no,use=reference
User-agent: GPTBot          Disallow: /
User-agent: ClaudeBot       Disallow: /
User-agent: Google-Extended Disallow: /
User-agent: CCBot           Disallow: /
User-agent: Bytespider      Disallow: /
User-agent: Amazonbot       Disallow: /
User-agent: Applebot-Extended  Disallow: /
User-agent: meta-externalagent Disallow: /
```

**Impact:** ClaudeBot (Claude), GPTBot (OpenAI), Google-Extended (Gemini grounding & training), and CCBot (Common Crawl — a dataset most LLMs train on) are all locked out. For a GEO strategy this is self-defeating: you are asking the AI engines not to know you exist.
**Nuance:** the *live-search* fetchers — `OAI-SearchBot` / `ChatGPT-User` (ChatGPT web search) and `PerplexityBot` — are **not** blocked, so real-time citation is still technically possible on those two. But the training/grounding crawlers, which drive "unprompted" brand recall, are shut out.
**Fix:** In Cloudflare, disable the AI-crawler block (Cloudflare Dashboard → your zone → **AI Audit / robots.txt "Manage AI bots"**, or turn off "Block AI bots" / the managed robots.txt feature). At minimum, allow `ClaudeBot`, `GPTBot`, `Google-Extended`, and `CCBot`. Decide `ai-train` deliberately, but for GEO you generally want `ai-input` and reference use permitted.

### 2. No content-level structured data for a Q&A-heavy consultancy
The site publishes extensive FAQ and step-by-step guide content (see the `延伸問答` Q&A block on blog posts, `常見問題` on `/consult/` and `/ireland-study-consultation/`) but there is **zero FAQPage, HowTo, or Service schema anywhere**. AI engines lean heavily on this markup to lift answer blocks verbatim. You have the content and none of the machine-readable signals.

## High Priority Issues (Fix Within 1 Week)

### 3. No `llms.txt` file
`/llms.txt` returns 404. This emerging standard gives AI systems a curated map of your best content. For a content-rich advisory site it's a cheap, high-leverage add.

### 4. Organization schema is a hollow shell
The `Organization` node exists but every entity-defining field is `null`:

```
name: 築夢愛爾國際留遊學顧問 Dream Eire International   ✓
logo: present                                          ✓
sameAs: null      ✗   email: null   ✗   telephone: null ✗
address: null     ✗   description: null ✗
```

Meanwhile the site *does* link Instagram, LinkedIn, and Threads in the footer — but they're absent from `sameAs`, so AI can't connect the social entities to the business. This is the single biggest quick win for entity recognition.

### 5. Author attribution is generic, wasting real E-E-A-T
The blog's greatest asset is that Alex and Arsha are real, named, credentialed experts writing from lived experience (e.g. "含 Alex 2023 年實際申請經驗"). Yet the `Person`/`author` schema resolves to a generic site account ("哩來愛爾蘭", `sameAs` → an old `lilaiireland-mhcjd.wordpress.com` blog, no `jobTitle`, no credentials). AI attributes the expertise to nobody.
**Fix:** Create real author profiles for Alex and Arsha (with `jobTitle`, `alumniOf` = UCD / Trinity College Dublin, `sameAs` → their LinkedIn), and assign posts to the actual author.

### 6. Near-zero brand authority footprint
A brand search (`哩來愛爾蘭` / `lilaiireland`) returns only competitors, never LiLai. No Wikipedia, no Reddit/PTT/Dcard threads, no YouTube presence surfaced. Brand mentions now correlate ~3x more strongly with AI citation than backlinks do (Ahrefs, Dec 2025), so this is a strategic gap, not just vanity.

## Medium Priority Issues (Fix Within 1 Month)

### 7. Duplicated H1 / heading hierarchy on every page
The site title `哩來愛爾蘭｜愛爾蘭留遊學代辦，在地學長姐陪你規劃語校與生活` is rendered as an `<h1>` on **every** page (likely the logo/header), so content pages carry 2–3 H1s (`/consult/` has 2, `/ireland-study-consultation/` has 3, blog posts have 2). This dilutes the topical signal of the real page heading. Convert the header/logo to non-heading markup and keep exactly one content-specific H1 per page.

### 8. WooCommerce shop has no Product/Offer schema
`/shop/` emits only `CollectionPage` + `BreadcrumbList`, no `Product`, `Offer`, or `AggregateRating`. If products/packages are sold there, they are invisible to AI shopping and comparison answers.

### 9. Thin cornerstone pages
- **Homepage:** ~318 CJK characters of body text — mostly hero imagery + CTAs. Very little extractable substance for the site's most-linked page.
- **`/recommended-ireland-language-schools/`:** ~153 CJK characters, 0 H2s — a page that should be a rich, comparison-table asset (exactly what AI cites) is nearly empty.

### 10. Missing entity/contact trust signals
No `mailto:` or `tel:` on the homepage, no LocalBusiness/ContactPoint data. Even for a remote consultancy, a clear contact block plus `ContactPoint` schema strengthens trust and entity completeness.

## Category Deep Dives

### AI Citability — 62/100
**Strengths:** The long-form blog is genuinely citation-ready. The 打工度假 guide runs ~6,400 CJK characters, structured as `第一章…第六章` plus a 10-question `延伸問答` block, packed with the specifics AI extracts: exact dates (11 月 1 日 registration), figures, week-count comparisons (25+8 vs working holiday), and named pitfalls. This is model GEO content.
**Weaknesses:** The money pages (`/consult/`, `/ireland-study-consultation/`) are decent (~2,300–2,600 chars) but the homepage and schools page are too thin to be quoted, and none of the Q&A content is wrapped in FAQ schema, so extraction is left to chance. Passage-level answer blocks ("What is X? — one-sentence answer") are the fastest lift.

### Brand Authority — 32/100
Young brand (WordPress content dated Feb–Aug 2026). Owns Instagram (`/lilaiireland/`), LinkedIn (`/company/lilaiireland`), and Threads (`@lilaiireland`), but no third-party validation appeared in search: competitors own the SERP, and there's no Wikipedia, Reddit, PTT, Dcard, or YouTube entity presence. AI entity recognition has little to anchor to. Priority: earn mentions on the platforms Taiwanese study-abroad seekers actually use (Dcard, PTT, YouTube, Threads) and get listed in study-abroad directories.

### Content E-E-A-T — 68/100
The highest-scoring dimension, and deservedly so. **Experience:** founders live in Ireland and write from personal application/relocation experience. **Expertise:** verifiable credentials (UCD MSc CS; TCD MSc Digital Marketing + TESOL). **Authoritativeness:** detailed About page, consistent authorial voice, terms/agreement page present. **Trust:** freshness is strong (content modified July–Aug 2026). The gap is purely *machine-readability* — the human E-E-A-T is excellent but the structured author attribution throws most of it away (see #5).

### Technical GEO — 48/100
**Good:** Server-side rendered (WordPress, no JS-dependency for content), HTTPS + Cloudflare, valid `sitemap_index.xml` (Rank Math), correct `canonical`, `lang="zh-TW"`, `robots: follow,index`. **Bad:** the AI-crawler block (Critical #1) is the dominant drag on this score, and there's no `llms.txt`. Fix the crawler policy and this category jumps ~25 points on its own.

### Schema & Structured Data — 55/100
Rank Math provides a competent baseline graph: `Organization`, `WebSite` + `SearchAction`, `Person`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `CollectionPage`, `ImageObject`. But it's generic-default: the Organization node is hollow (#4), there's no `FAQPage`/`HowTo`/`Service`/`Product` (#2, #8), and the Person node is a placeholder. All fixable within Rank Math's UI + a few JSON-LD snippets.

### Platform Optimization — 42/100
- **Perplexity:** accessible — `PerplexityBot` is not blocked. Best near-term citation channel.
- **ChatGPT (web search):** live fetchers `OAI-SearchBot`/`ChatGPT-User` not blocked, so real-time citation is possible; but `GPTBot` blocked means no training-time brand memory.
- **Claude:** `ClaudeBot` blocked — effectively invisible.
- **Google AI Overviews / Gemini:** `Google-Extended` blocked (no Gemini grounding/training); AIO can still draw on the standard index since `Googlebot` isn't blocked, but you've opted out of the generative layer.
Weak brand authority caps all platforms regardless.
